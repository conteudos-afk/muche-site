The Muche website sets no cookies. None: not our own, not for analytics, not for advertising, not from social networks. That is why you do not see a cookie banner when you arrive: there was nothing to ask you.

This page explains what is nonetheless stored in your browser, and the one link to a third party that the site has, which only switches on if you ask for it.

A cookie is a small file that a website stores on your device and that is sent back to it each time you return. It is used to recognise you: to keep you signed in, to count visits, to show adverts. Our site uses none.

## What we store in your browser

Two things, and neither leaves your device: the language you chose and, if you play, your progress in the game.

### Your language

- **Name:** `muche-lang`
- **Where:** your browser's `localStorage`. It is not a cookie, but the law treats it in the same way, so we tell you about it.
- **Content:** `pt` or `en`.
- **When it is stored:** only when you click PT or EN on the site. If you never choose, nothing is stored and the site uses your browser's language.
- **What for:** so that the site opens in the language you chose next time.
- **Duration:** it does not expire. It stays until you delete it.
- **Where it goes:** nowhere. It stays on your device and is never sent to any server, ours or anyone else's.

**Why we do not ask for consent.** It is a preference that you choose yourself, and it exists only so that the site works the way you asked. Portuguese Law 41/2004, which implements the European directive on privacy in electronic communications, does not require consent where storage is strictly necessary to provide a service the user has expressly requested. Choosing a language is exactly that.

**How to delete it.** In your browser settings, under site data (or "cookies and site data"), remove the data for muche.pt. The site goes back to using your browser's language.

### The game

At [/jogo/](/jogo/) there is a game, *Muche vs. Stressalot*, which stores what you have done so that you do not start from scratch every time.

- **Names:** `muche.prog` and `muche.sound`
- **Where:** your browser's `localStorage`, same as the language.
- **Content:** your best score on each level, the levels you finished, the socks you won, and whether sound is on.
- **When it is stored:** while you play. If you never open the game page, nothing is stored.
- **What for:** so the game remembers your progress and your sound preference.
- **Duration:** it does not expire. It stays until you delete it.
- **Where it goes:** nowhere. The game runs entirely on your device and talks to no server, ours or anyone else's.

**Why we do not ask for consent.** The same reasoning as for the language: it is the game doing what you asked of it by playing. None of it identifies you, and none of it is shared with anyone.

**How to delete it.** Inside the game itself, with the "Apagar progresso" button. Or, like everything else, in your browser settings.

## The one link to a third party

In the "Work" section, on the desktop version, there is an image of a laptop that can show the live website of Teentac, a client of ours. By default it shows only the image. Teentac's website is loaded only if you click the "Load live preview" button.

- **If you click:** teentac.pt receives your IP address and may store its own cookies on your device. We do not control which cookies they are or what they do: that is down to Teentac and that website's own policy.
- **If you do not click:** nothing. Your browser does not contact teentac.pt.
- **To undo it:** reload the page. Any cookies Teentac has already stored can be cleared in your browser settings, where you can also block third-party cookies.

On phones and tablets there is no button: you only see the image.

## Everything else

- Fonts, videos and images come from the site itself. We do not load fonts, scripts, videos or images from other domains.
- We use no analytics tools, tracking pixels or advertising.
- The links to Facebook, Instagram and LinkedIn are ordinary links, with no buttons or embeds: you only leave our site if you click, and on those sites their rules apply.
