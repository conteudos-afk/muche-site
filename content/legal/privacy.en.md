## What happens when you open the site

### Server logs

The site is hosted on Cloudflare (Cloudflare Pages). Whenever you open a page, Cloudflare's servers receive and record your IP address and the details of the request, such as the page you asked for, the date and time and the type of browser. They are used to keep the site running and to protect it from attacks and abuse.

- **Legal basis:** our legitimate interest in keeping the site secure and working (Article 6(1)(f) of the GDPR).
- **Retention:** these logs are kept by Cloudflare, and it is Cloudflare that decides for how long. We do not set that period.

### When you write to us

The site has no forms: contact is by email. If you write to conteudos@muche.pt (or click an email address on the site), your message arrives in our mailbox, which is hosted on Google Workspace. We keep your email address and whatever you write to us, so that we can reply.

Nothing else happens to it. Messages do not go into a CRM, onto a mailing list or into a database: they stay in the mailbox.

- **Legal basis:** your request, when you write to ask for a proposal or a service (Article 6(1)(b) of the GDPR); in other cases, our legitimate interest in replying to people who write to us (point (f)).
- **Retention:** we keep messages for as long as the relationship justifies it, that is, while there is a conversation, a project or a legal obligation to meet. We have not set a period in days or years, because it depends on the case, and we would rather say so than make up a number. If you want us to delete a conversation, ask: we will delete it, unless the law requires us to keep something (invoicing documents, for example).

### The Teentac preview

In the "Work" section, on the desktop version, the site shows an image of a laptop. There is a button that loads Teentac's live website inside it. Teentac is a client of ours, and that website is not ours.

**Your browser contacts teentac.pt only after you click that button.** When you do, teentac.pt receives your IP address and the usual details of a request, and may store its own cookies on your device. That no longer goes through us: it is handled by Teentac, under that website's own privacy policy. If you do not click the button, none of this happens. On phones and tablets there is no button: you only see the image.

- **Legal basis:** your consent, which you give by clicking the button (Article 6(1)(a) of the GDPR). You can withdraw it whenever you like: reload the page and Teentac's website is no longer open. Any cookies it has already stored can be cleared in your browser settings.

### Cookies and browser storage

The site sets no cookies. It only stores the language you choose in your browser, and that stays with you. The details are in the [cookie policy](/en/cookies/).

### Automated decisions and profiling

We make no automated decisions about you and we do not profile you. There is no behavioural analysis, no targeted advertising and nothing like it.

## Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you can:

- find out whether we process data about you and obtain access to it;
- ask for incorrect data to be corrected;
- ask for your data to be erased;
- ask for processing to be restricted;
- object to processing that is based on our legitimate interest;
- receive your data in a common format, where that applies;
- withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting anything done before you withdrew it.

To exercise any of these rights, write to [conteudos@muche.pt](mailto:conteudos@muche.pt). We reply within one month. We may ask you to confirm who you are before we deal with the request. Server logs are kept by Cloudflare, so for those we may need to contact it.

If you think we are not handling your data properly, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (CNPD), the supervisory authority in Portugal: [www.cnpd.pt](https://www.cnpd.pt).

You are not required to give us any personal data in order to view the site. If you want us to reply by email, your address is unavoidable; the rest is whatever you choose to tell us.
